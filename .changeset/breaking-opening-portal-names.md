The `a-doorhole` primitive and `doorhole` component are now `a-opening` and `opening`, and `a-doorlink` and `doorlink` are now `a-portal` and `portal`. An opening element's `getDoorlink()` is now `getPortal()`. Rename them in your markup and scripts; the old names are not registered.
